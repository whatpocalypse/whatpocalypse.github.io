The channel

What if the Sun went out? Whatpocalypse takes impossible “what if” scenarios and follows them through, one step at a time, with real science.

A new Short every day and a long video every week, told with science-based, code-generated 3D animation. We look at what happens to the world, and what happens to you: your phone, your tap water, the shelves at the store, the neighbors down the street.

  • Real science

    Every claim has a source

    Each scenario is researched first. Estimates are labeled on screen and the sources are listed in the description.

  • Made in code

    Every frame is built, not generated

    The 3D animation is made with Blender and Python. Music and sound effects are synthesized in code. No AI-generated images or video.

  • Human scale

    The world, and your street

    Each stop on the timeline shows what changes for you, for the people around you and for the jobs that keep a city running.
